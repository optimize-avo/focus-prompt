# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Marketers, Brand Managers, SEO Professionals, and Content Creators who want to optimize their brand's visibility in AI chatbot responses. They use this tool to discover what unbranded prompts might lead AI systems to recommend or mention their brand, then generate and score those prompts for implementation.

**Context**: Users are typically in a strategic planning phase, researching how AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, etc.) currently surface brands in their niche, and what prompts they should optimize for.

**Job to be done**: "I want to understand how AI chatbots discover and recommend brands like mine, so I can create content that increases my brand's visibility in AI responses."

## Product Purpose

Focus Prompt is an AI Brand Visibility Research Tool that predicts and generates unbranded prompts that AI chatbots might use to find and mention a user's brand. The tool automates the research-discovery-generation-scoring pipeline to help brands optimize for AI search visibility.

**Success looks like**: Users can run a complete pipeline (Research → Discover → Generate → Score → Export) and get actionable, scored prompts they can use to create content that improves their brand's AI visibility.

## Brand Personality

**Professional, Efficient, Data-driven**

- **Professional**: Clean, serious interface that respects the user's expertise
- **Efficient**: No unnecessary steps, clear workflow, fast results
- **Data-driven**: Decisions and outputs backed by research and scoring metrics

## Anti-references

- **Too playful or gamified**: No badges, achievements, confetti, or game-like elements
- **Overly colorful**: Avoid bright, saturated color schemes; prefer muted, professional tones
- **Cluttered layouts**: No information overload; prioritize clarity and hierarchy
- **Marketing hype**: No exaggerated claims, flashy animations, or sales-y language

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness**: Interface should be immediately understandable without explanation
2. **Progressive disclosure**: Show what's needed now, hide complexity until relevant
3. **Data-first presentation**: Lead with numbers, scores, and metrics; support with text
4. **Workflow coherence**: Each step should clearly connect to the next
5. **Professional restraint**: Subtle interactions, no gratuitous animations or effects

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance target
- Support for reduced motion preferences
- Clear color contrast (minimum 4.5:1 for body text)
- Keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader compatible
